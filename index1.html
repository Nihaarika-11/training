I-GAARD

Intelligent AI-Driven Geofence Animal Detection and Real-Time Alert System

I-GAARD is an AI-based wildlife monitoring and alert-system concept designed to help reduce human–wildlife conflict in forest-edge areas. It combines AI-enabled camera monitoring, IoT sensors, geofencing, long-range communication, and real-time alerts to support villagers, tourists, and forest authorities.

01 · DetectAI-enabled camera monitoring and sensor inputs.
02 · LocateGeofencing to monitor virtual boundaries.
